A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To my MAMA, with all my love 💌

To my favourite person, MAMA ♡

My dearest MAMA,

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️ Un life-la nee innum neraya happiness, success, peace ellame experience pannanum. Un smile eppovum ippadiye irukkanum.

Un kooda pesura chinna chinna moments kooda enakku romba special. Sometimes words-la solla mudiyatha alavukku nee enakku important. Un presence en ordinary days-a kooda beautiful-a maathidum.

Namma life always perfect-a irukkanum nu naan expect pannala. Happy days irukkum, difficult days-um irukkum. Aana understanding-oda, honesty-oda, love-oda namma rendu perum orutharukku oruthar support-a irukkanum nu aasai paduren.

MAMA, nee un dreams ellame achieve pannanum. Nee tired-a irukkumbodhu rest edukkanum, sad-a irukkumbodhu un feelings-a share pannanum, happy-a irukkumbodhu en kooda celebrate pannanum.

Indha universe-la evlo stars irundhaalum, enakku special-a theriyura star nee dhaan. Thank you for being part of my life. Un birthday-la mattum illa, ordinary days-layum nee loved and appreciated-a feel pannanum.

I love you, MAMA. More than these little words can explain. ♡

Forever with love,
Your Thilaga 💗
